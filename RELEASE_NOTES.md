# SEFAR NARATOR V1.0 — Continuous Story Presentation

This V1.0 refinement keeps the complete story on one continuous page.

## Change
- Removed chapter-by-chapter pagination from the story display.
- Removed Previous/Next chapter controls and story progress pagination.
- The complete narrative is now presented as one continuous reading flow on the page.
- Paragraph spacing remains visually clear for comfortable reading.
- Scientific facts, discovery, questions, activity and related discoveries remain below the complete story.

## Validation

- ZIP integrity check passed with `unzip -t`.
